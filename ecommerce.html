Why migrate from PrestaShop to Shopify

PrestaShop stores often mean self-managed servers, manual security patching, and plugin conflicts. Shopify removes that overhead while giving you a faster checkout, built-in fraud protection, and an app ecosystem that scales with the business.

⚡

Faster, more reliable storefront

Shopify's hosted infrastructure handles traffic spikes and checkout load that self-hosted PrestaShop servers often struggle with during sales events.

🔒

Less security overhead

No more manual PrestaShop core and module patching — PCI compliance, SSL, and platform security are handled by Shopify directly.

🧩

Larger app & integration ecosystem

Connect marketing, inventory, and support tools through Shopify's app store instead of custom PrestaShop module development.

The migration process, step by step

Every step below runs through official PrestaShop and Shopify APIs — not manual CSV re-entry — so product data, customer records, and design assets transfer accurately.

Discovery & store audit

We review your PrestaShop store's structure, product catalog, customer database, current URLs, and existing search rankings before touching anything.

API-based data export from PrestaShop

Products, categories, customers, and orders are extracted through PrestaShop's Webservice API, preserving relationships between data instead of flattening them into spreadsheets.

PrestaShop Webservice API

Product & catalog migration

Products, variants, images, collections, and inventory levels are pushed into Shopify, mapping PrestaShop's attribute structure onto Shopify's product model.

Shopify Admin API

Customer & order data migration

Customer accounts, order history, and saved addresses move over via API, so returning customers can log in and see their past orders without re-registering.

Design & theme rebuild

Your storefront is rebuilt in Shopify's Liquid theme framework, matching your existing brand and layout — or improving on it — rather than using a generic template.

SEO preservation & 301 redirects

Every existing PrestaShop URL is mapped to its new Shopify equivalent with 301 redirects, so backlinks and indexed pages carry their ranking value over instead of resetting.

Testing & quality assurance

Checkout, payment gateways, shipping rules, tax settings, and mobile responsiveness are tested end-to-end before the new store goes live.

Performance optimization & secure launch

Page speed, image compression, SSL, and Shopify security settings are configured, then DNS is switched over with minimal downtime.

What actually moves, and how

"API-based migration" isn't just a buzzword — here's specifically what each API handles during a PrestaShop to Shopify move, so you know exactly what's being transferred and how.

🗂️

PrestaShop Webservice API

Reads your store's products, categories, combinations (variants), stock levels, customers, addresses, and order history directly from the database layer — not from an export file that can go stale mid-migration.

🛍️

Shopify Admin API

Writes products, variants, images, collections, and inventory into Shopify in the correct structure, including metafields for anything Shopify's default product model doesn't cover out of the box.

👥

Customer & order endpoints

Customer accounts and their order history are created in Shopify with matching emails, so returning customers can request a password reset and see their full order history — no re-registration needed.

Frequently asked questions

The questions we get asked most before starting a PrestaShop to Shopify project.

How long does a PrestaShop to Shopify migration take?

Most migrations take 1 to 3 weeks depending on catalog size, number of customers, and how much custom design work the new Shopify theme needs.

Will I lose my Google rankings when migrating?

Not if 301 redirects are set up correctly. Every old PrestaShop URL is mapped to its new Shopify URL so search engines carry over existing rankings and backlinks instead of starting from zero.

Is customer data safely transferred?

Yes — customer accounts, order history and addresses are transferred via API rather than manual re-entry, keeping data accurate and avoiding disruption for returning customers.

Do you migrate stores for businesses in Italy and Spain?

Yes — VortexDigitalAI supports PrestaShop to Shopify migrations for businesses in Italy and Spain specifically, as well as Pakistan, UAE, USA, UK and worldwide.

What happens to my existing product reviews?

Product reviews are exported alongside product data and re-imported into a Shopify reviews app, so social proof built up on the old store isn't lost.

Can the store stay live during migration?

Yes — data is migrated to a staging Shopify store first, tested fully, and DNS is only switched over once everything is verified, keeping downtime to minutes, not days.
